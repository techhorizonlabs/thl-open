# Reference data: correlations + presence-building playbook

Background for the scoring weights and recommendations. Cite the correlation table when a client
asks *why* YouTube and Reddit outrank their backlink profile.

## Correlation strengths (Ahrefs study, Dec 2025, 75K brands)

| Signal | Correlation with AI citation | Traditional SEO value |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube mentions | ~0.737 | Low (not a ranking factor) |
| Reddit mentions | High (exact coefficient not published) | Low |
| Wikipedia presence | High | Moderate (trust signal) |
| LinkedIn presence | Moderate | Low |
| Domain Rating | ~0.266 | Very High |
| Backlink count | ~0.266 | Very High |
| Organic traffic | Moderate | Very High |

**Key insight:** the signals that matter most for AI visibility (YouTube, Reddit) are almost
irrelevant in traditional SEO, and the signals that matter most for traditional SEO (backlinks,
DR) are weak predictors of AI visibility. This is why GEO needs a fundamentally different
optimization strategy from SEO.

## Platform-specific tips for building presence

Use these to turn the report's weakest-platform findings into concrete recommendations.

**YouTube quick wins:**
- Create a channel and upload 3-5 explainer videos on your core topics.
- Put the brand name in video titles, descriptions, and spoken content.
- Pursue guest appearances on relevant industry channels.
- Make comparison / "alternatives" videos — these get cited by AI for comparison queries.

**Reddit quick wins:**
- Identify 3-5 subreddits where the target audience is active.
- Participate authentically — do not shill; communities detect and punish it.
- Run an AMA if appropriate.
- Monitor and respond to brand mentions.
- Post genuinely helpful content that naturally surfaces the brand's expertise.

**Wikipedia strategy:**
- Hire a Wikipedia-knowledgeable consultant — do NOT edit your own article (conflict of interest).
- Build notability first through press coverage, academic citations, and industry recognition.
- Complete the Wikidata entry even without a Wikipedia article.
- Contribute to industry articles where the brand can be naturally cited as a source.

**LinkedIn quick wins:**
- Complete the company page and post regularly.
- Have leadership post thought-leadership content weekly.
- Publish LinkedIn articles where the brand has unique expertise.
- Engage industry discussions to raise visibility in professional contexts.
